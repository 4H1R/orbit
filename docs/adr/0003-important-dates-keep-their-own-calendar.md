# Important dates keep their own Calendar

An Important date is stored as (Calendar, month, day, optional year) and recurs in that Calendar. It is not stored as a `LocalDate`, and it is not converted into one global calendar. A fixed Solar Hijri day moves ±1 against Gregorian from year to year (5 Aban is 27 Oct in most years but 26 Oct in 1403, 1407 and 1408), so a Shamsi birthday stored as a Gregorian date would sometimes be celebrated on the wrong day. A year-less date also has no `LocalDate` at all. Collapsing to one calendar would be simpler, but it throws away which Calendar the user meant, and that can't be recovered later. Each occurrence is resolved to a `LocalDate` only when it's needed; on a day the month doesn't have that year, it falls on the month's last day (30 Esfand → 29 Esfand, 29 Feb → 28 Feb).
